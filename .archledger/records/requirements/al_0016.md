## Requirement

toktrail must compute actual and virtual costs from user-configurable pricing definitions
in TOML. It must never invent cost estimates or call external pricing APIs.

## Rationale

- Pricing varies by provider, model, and context window.
- Users need to see what they are spending.
- External pricing APIs would violate the local-only privacy guarantee.

## Source

- `toktrail/costing.py` — cost computation engine
- `toktrail/config_parts/parse_pricing.py` — pricing config parsing
- `~/.config/toktrail/prices.toml` — user pricing definitions
